HOA House Rules Template

A complete, customizable set of house rules covering enforcement, common areas, residences, pets, vehicles, trash, and architectural restrictions. Copy and adapt these rules for your community.

Before You Adopt: House rules must be consistent with your CC&Rs, bylaws, and state law. Have your association's attorney review any rules before the board votes to adopt them. Rules that conflict with governing documents or state statute may be unenforceable.

HOUSE RULES AND REGULATIONS [Association Full Legal Name] [Association Address] [City, State, ZIP] Adopted by the Board of Directors on [Date of Board Vote] Effective Date: [Effective Date] These House Rules and Regulations ("Rules") are adopted pursuant to the authority granted to the Board of Directors ("Board") under the Declaration of Covenants, Conditions, and Restrictions ("CC&Rs") and the Bylaws of [Association Name]. These Rules supplement, but do not replace, the CC&Rs and Bylaws. In the event of a conflict between these Rules and the CC&Rs or Bylaws, the CC&Rs and Bylaws shall control. ======================================== SECTION A: RULES ENFORCEMENT & FINE SCHEDULE ======================================== A.1 DEFINITIONS For purposes of these Rules: (a) "Owner" means any person or entity holding recorded title to a lot or unit within the Association. (b) "Tenant" means any person occupying a lot or unit under a lease or rental agreement with an Owner. (c) "Guest" means any person invited onto the property by an Owner, Tenant, or Occupant. (d) "Occupant" means any person residing in a unit, whether as Owner, Tenant, or authorized household member. (e) "Board" means the Board of Directors of the Association. (f) "Managing Agent" means the property management company or individual retained by the Board to manage the day-to-day operations of the Association. A.2 VIOLATION NOTICE PROCEDURE (a) Upon observing or receiving a report of a violation, the Board or Managing Agent shall issue a written Notice of Violation to the Owner of the unit in question. (b) The Notice shall identify the specific Rule violated, describe the nature of the violation, and state the date by which the violation must be cured. The cure period shall be no less than [14 / 30] days from the date of the Notice, except in cases of emergency or safety hazards. (c) The Owner shall have the right to request a hearing before the Board prior to the imposition of any fine. The request must be submitted in writing within [14] days of receiving the Notice of Violation. (d) At the hearing, the Owner (or Tenant, if applicable) may present evidence, witnesses, and arguments in their defense. The Board shall issue a written decision within [14] days of the hearing. A.3 FINE SCHEDULE Offense Fine Amount ------- ----------- 1st Offense Written Warning (no fine) 2nd Offense $50.00 3rd Offense $100.00 Subsequent Offenses $200.00 per occurrence Continuing Violation $25.00 per day until cured Fines are assessed per unit. Each separate Rule violated constitutes a separate offense. Fines are due within [30] days of the date of the fine notice or Board decision, whichever is later. A.4 APPEALS PROCESS (a) Any Owner who has been fined may appeal the decision in writing to the Board within [30] days of receiving the fine notice or hearing decision. (b) The Board shall review the appeal at the next regularly scheduled Board meeting and issue a written decision within [14] days thereafter. (c) The Board's decision on appeal shall be final. Owners retain all rights to pursue legal remedies as permitted by state law. ======================================== SECTION B: GENERAL RULES ======================================== B.1 OWNER RESPONSIBILITY Each Owner is responsible for ensuring that all Tenants, Guests, and Occupants of their unit comply with these Rules, the CC&Rs, and the Bylaws. Violations by a Tenant, Guest, or Occupant shall be treated as violations by the Owner for purposes of enforcement and fines. B.2 REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS (a) Owners must notify the Managing Agent in writing at least [14] days before any move-in or move-out. (b) Move-in and move-out activities are permitted only during the hours of [8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Saturday]. No move-in or move-out activities are permitted on Sundays or federal holidays. (c) Owners who lease their unit must provide the Managing Agent with a copy of the lease and the Tenant's contact information within [14] days of lease execution. B.3 TENANT OBLIGATIONS All Tenants must receive a copy of these Rules, the CC&Rs, and the Bylaws from the Owner prior to or at the time of lease execution. Owners shall include a provision in their lease requiring Tenant compliance with all Association governing documents. B.4 VACANT PROPERTY REQUIREMENTS (a) Owners of vacant units must maintain the exterior, landscaping, and any assigned common area in the same condition as occupied units. (b) Owners of vacant units must provide the Managing Agent with current contact information and the name of a local responsible party who can be contacted in case of emergency. B.5 INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS Each Owner shall maintain insurance coverage as required by the CC&Rs and applicable state law. Proof of insurance may be requested by the Board or Managing Agent at any time. ======================================== SECTION C: COMMON AREA RESTRICTIONS ======================================== C.1 No alterations, additions, or modifications to any common area without prior written approval of the Board. C.2 No bicycles, scooters, skateboards, or similar personal transportation devices shall be left unattended in common areas. Items left in violation of this Rule may be removed and stored at the Owner's expense. C.3 No fireworks, open flames, or incendiary devices of any kind are permitted in common areas, except in designated barbecue areas with proper supervision. C.4 No loitering in common areas during quiet hours (10:00 PM to 7:00 AM). Common areas are for transit and designated recreational use only during these hours. C.5 No personal property, furniture, or storage items shall be placed or stored in common areas, hallways, stairwells, or breezeways at any time. C.6 No smoking in any enclosed common area, including but not limited to lobbies, hallways, stairwells, elevators, clubhouse, and fitness rooms. C.7 No soliciting, canvassing, or distribution of materials on Association property without prior written approval of the Board. This does not apply to communications protected by applicable state or federal law. C.8 POOL AND RECREATION AREA RULES (a) Pool hours: [8:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily]. (b) No lifeguard is on duty. All persons use the pool at their own risk. (c) Children under [14] years of age must be accompanied by a responsible adult. (d) No glass containers in the pool area. (e) No running, diving, or rough play. (f) Shower before entering the pool. (g) Maximum occupancy: [number] persons. (h) Guest limit: [2 / 4] guests per unit at any one time. C.9 PLAYGROUND RULES (a) Playground is for children ages [2 to 12] only. (b) Adult supervision required at all times. (c) Playground hours: [8:00 AM to dusk]. (d) No food or beverages on playground equipment. ======================================== SECTION D: INDIVIDUAL RESIDENCE RULES ======================================== D.1 EXTERIOR MODIFICATIONS All exterior modifications, including but not limited to paint colors, fencing, awnings, shutters, doors, windows, and landscaping changes, require prior written approval from the Architectural Committee. Applications must be submitted to the Managing Agent and will be reviewed in accordance with the Architectural Guidelines. D.2 FENCING STANDARDS All fencing must conform to the approved styles, materials, and height restrictions set forth in the Architectural Guidelines. Maximum fence height: [6 feet for rear yard, 4 feet for front yard], unless otherwise specified in the CC&Rs or required by local code. D.3 LANDSCAPING MAINTENANCE (a) Owners are responsible for maintaining the landscaping on their lot in a neat, trimmed, and healthy condition. (b) Lawns must be mowed regularly and may not exceed [6 inches] in height. (c) Dead plants, trees, and shrubs must be removed and replaced within [30] days. (d) Xeriscaping and drought-tolerant landscaping are permitted with prior Architectural Committee approval. D.4 NOISE RESTRICTIONS Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM daily. During quiet hours, noise from any unit shall not be audible beyond the boundaries of that unit. Construction and power tool use is limited to [8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Saturday]. No construction or power tool use on Sundays or federal holidays. D.5 HOLIDAY DECORATIONS Holiday decorations may be displayed no more than 30 days before the holiday and must be removed within 14 days after the holiday. Decorations must not damage common elements, create a safety hazard, or include sound devices audible outside the unit. D.6 WINDOW TREATMENTS All windows visible from the exterior must have appropriate window treatments. Aluminum foil, newspaper, bedsheets, and similar materials are not permitted as window coverings. Temporary coverings during a move-in period may be used for no more than [14] days. D.7 SATELLITE DISHES AND ANTENNAS Satellite dishes one meter or less in diameter and television antennas may be installed in accordance with FCC OTARD rules (47 C.F.R. Section 1.4000). Dishes and antennas must be installed in the least visible location that permits acceptable signal quality. Installations on common elements require Board approval. D.8 COMMERCIAL USE No unit shall be used for commercial purposes that generate regular customer or client traffic, create noise, odors, or other disturbances, or are inconsistent with the residential character of the community. Home offices with no external impact are permitted. ======================================== SECTION E: PET RULES ======================================== E.1 A maximum of [2] pets per household are permitted. Permitted pets include dogs and cats only. Fish in aquariums are not counted toward the pet limit. E.2 All pets must be registered with the Managing Agent. Registration requires proof of current vaccinations and applicable local licensing. E.3 All pets must be on a leash no longer than [6 feet] at all times when in common areas. Pets are not permitted in the pool area, playground, or clubhouse. E.4 Owners must immediately clean up after their pets in all common areas and on neighboring properties. Failure to clean up after a pet is subject to the fine schedule in Section A.3. E.5 No dangerous, exotic, venomous, or wild animals are permitted. Breed restrictions, if any, are set forth in the CC&Rs. Assistance animals and service animals are exempt from pet restrictions as required by federal and state fair housing laws. E.6 BARKING AND NOISE COMPLAINTS (a) Persistent or excessive barking, howling, or other animal noise that disturbs other residents is a violation. (b) Three (3) documented complaints from separate households within a [12-month] period will trigger a mandatory hearing before the Board. (c) The Board may require the Owner to take corrective action, including but not limited to professional training, and may impose fines per the schedule in Section A.3. ======================================== SECTION F: VEHICLES & PARKING ======================================== F.1 Each unit is assigned [one (1)] parking space. Additional spaces may be available on a first-come, first-served basis if capacity allows, subject to Board policies. F.2 Guest parking is limited to designated guest spaces only. Guest vehicles may not remain parked for more than 72 consecutive hours without prior written authorization from the Managing Agent. F.3 No vehicle maintenance, repair, or washing (other than with a bucket and sponge) is permitted in parking areas or on community roads. F.4 INOPERABLE AND UNREGISTERED VEHICLES (a) Vehicles that are inoperable, unregistered, or have expired registration are not permitted on Association property. (b) The Managing Agent shall issue a 72-hour written notice to the Owner. (c) If the vehicle is not removed or brought into compliance within 72 hours, it may be towed at the vehicle Owner's expense. F.5 No commercial vehicles (other than standard passenger vehicles with minor commercial markings), boats, trailers, recreational vehicles (RVs), or oversized vehicles may be parked on Association property without prior written Board approval. F.6 The speed limit on all community roads is 15 MPH. Reckless driving or excessive speed is a violation subject to the fine schedule in Section A.3. ======================================== SECTION G: TRASH & RECYCLING ======================================== G.1 All household trash must be placed in sealed bags inside approved trash containers. Containers must be stored out of public view except during designated collection periods. G.2 No dumping of large items, furniture, appliances, or construction debris in dumpsters or common areas. Large item disposal must be scheduled through [the Managing Agent / the local waste hauler]. G.3 Recycling participation is encouraged in accordance with local ordinances. Recyclable materials should be separated and placed in designated recycling containers where provided. G.4 Dumpster and trash collection areas must remain clean. Owners and Tenants who use these areas are responsible for cleaning up any spillage immediately. G.5 Trash containers shall be placed at the curb or designated collection point no earlier than [5:00 PM the evening before] collection and must be retrieved and stored out of view no later than [9:00 PM on collection day]. ======================================== SECTION H: ARCHITECTURAL RESTRICTIONS ======================================== H.1 All exterior modifications, additions, or improvements require prior written approval from the Architectural Committee BEFORE any work begins. This includes but is not limited to painting, roofing, siding, windows, doors, fencing, decking, patios, driveways, and landscaping changes. H.2 SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS Applications must include: (a) A description of the proposed modification (b) Detailed plans or drawings (c) Specification of materials, colors, and dimensions (d) Proposed timeline for completion (e) Name and license number of the contractor (if applicable) H.3 COMMITTEE RESPONSE The Architectural Committee shall respond in writing within 30 calendar days of receiving a complete application. If no written response is received within 30 days, the application is deemed DENIED. The Owner must resubmit or contact the Committee for status. H.4 SOLAR PANEL INSTALLATION Solar panel installations must comply with applicable state solar access laws. The Association may impose reasonable restrictions on placement and aesthetics that do not significantly increase cost or decrease efficiency, as permitted by state law. Owners must submit an application per Section H.2 prior to installation. H.5 SATELLITE DISHES Satellite dishes one meter (39.37 inches) or less in diameter are permitted under FCC Over-the-Air Reception Devices (OTARD) rules (47 C.F.R. Section 1.4000). The Association may not prohibit, restrict, or delay the installation of these devices, but may impose reasonable, non-cost-increasing placement preferences. H.6 UNAPPROVED MODIFICATIONS Modifications made without prior Architectural Committee approval are subject to enforcement action under Section A. The Board may require the Owner to restore the property to its prior condition at the Owner's sole expense, in addition to any fines imposed. ======================================== These Rules are subject to amendment by the Board of Directors in accordance with the CC&Rs and Bylaws. Tips for Adopting House Rules

1. Review Your Governing Documents First

House rules cannot contradict your CC&Rs or bylaws. Before drafting or adopting any rules, read through your Declaration and Bylaws carefully. Rules that conflict with higher governing documents are unenforceable and can expose the Association to legal liability.

2. Check Your State's HOA Laws

Every state has different requirements for how house rules can be adopted, amended, and enforced. Some states require a membership vote for certain types of rules. Others require specific notice periods or hearing procedures before fines can be imposed. Know what your state requires before the Board acts.

3. Get Homeowner Input

Rules that are developed with homeowner input are more likely to be followed and less likely to face legal challenges. Consider distributing a draft for comment before the Board votes. A 30-day comment period is a common and reasonable practice. Rules imposed without any community input often breed resentment and noncompliance.

4. Have an Attorney Review

An HOA attorney can identify rules that conflict with state or federal law (such as fair housing laws, FCC antenna rules, or state solar access statutes) before they become a liability. Attorney review of a house rules document typically costs $500 to $1,500 depending on complexity and is well worth the investment.

5. Vote and Record Properly

Follow the exact procedure outlined in your bylaws for adopting rules. This typically requires a Board vote at a properly noticed meeting with a quorum present. Record the vote in the meeting minutes, including who voted for and against. Procedural errors in adoption can make the entire set of rules unenforceable.

6. Distribute to All Residents

Once adopted, distribute the rules to every Owner and require Owners to provide a copy to their Tenants. Keep a record of distribution. Rules that residents never received are difficult to enforce. Many associations require a signed acknowledgment of receipt, which provides documentation if enforcement becomes necessary.
